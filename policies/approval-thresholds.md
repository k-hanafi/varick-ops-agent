# Invoice Approval Thresholds

Invoices that pass all reconciliation checks may be auto-approved for payment
**only if the invoice amount is below $10,000**.

Any invoice with an amount of **$10,000 or more requires explicit VP approval**
before payment. These invoices must be placed on hold and routed to the
responsible VP, regardless of whether the invoice matches its purchase order.

Auto-approval is never permitted for invoices at or above the $10,000 threshold.
